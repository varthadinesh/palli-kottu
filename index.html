The goodness of groundnuts

Groundnuts are nutrient-dense and can be enjoyed as part of a varied and balanced diet. Approximate values per 100 g of raw peanuts:

~26 gprotein
~8.5 gdietary fibre
~49 gfat, mostly unsaturated
~567 kcalenergy
Plant protein
A natural source of plant-based protein, useful in vegetarian meals.
Fibre
Groundnuts provide dietary fibre, which adds to a feeling of fullness.
Unsaturated fats
Most of the fat in groundnuts is unsaturated, as part of their natural profile.
Vitamin E
Groundnuts and groundnut oil naturally contain vitamin E.
Niacin & folate
Naturally contain B vitamins that play a role in normal everyday metabolism.
Magnesium
A mineral found naturally in groundnuts, along with others such as phosphorus.
Lasting energy
Energy-dense and satisfying, a practical choice for snacks and travel.
Plant compounds
Groundnuts, especially the skins, contain naturally occurring plant compounds.

Nutrition values are approximate (USDA FoodData Central, raw peanuts) and vary by variety and processing. This information is educational and is not medical advice. Contains peanuts.

In the kitchen

Simple ways to use Palli Kottu peanuts and oil every day.

Roasted peanut chutney

Dry-roast Palli Kottu peanuts, grind with green chilli, garlic, tamarind and salt. Serve with idli or dosa.

Sundal & boiled peanuts

Boil peanuts with salt, then temper with mustard, curry leaves and a little peanut oil.

Groundnut chikki

Melt jaggery to a firm-ball stage, stir in roasted peanuts, set and cut into pieces.

Tempering with peanut oil

Heat the oil, add mustard and cumin seeds, then pour over dals, rice and chutneys for aroma.

Crisp pakodas & fritters

Peanut oil is a familiar choice for frying in Indian kitchens, and adds its own flavour.

Peanut masala

Toss roasted peanuts with chopped onion, tomato, lemon and chaat masala for a quick snack.

Storage and handling

  • Store peanuts airtight. Keep them in a dry, airtight container away from moisture and direct sun.
  • Keep oil cool and sealed. Store the oil in a cool, dark place with the cap closed tightly after each use.
  • Use a dry spoon. Moisture speeds up spoilage in both peanuts and oil.
  • Roast in small batches. Dry-roast on low heat and stir often for even colour and flavour.

Questions

What products does Palli Kottu offer?

We focus on Premium Peanuts and Wood Pressed Peanut Oil.

What is wood pressed peanut oil?

Peanut oil produced using a traditional wood pressing approach, focused on the natural character, taste and aroma of groundnuts.

How is wood pressed oil different from refined oil?

Wood pressed oil is made by slow pressing and filtering, so it keeps more of the groundnut's natural aroma and taste. Refined oils usually go through further industrial processing and taste more neutral.

What can the oil be used for?

Everyday cooking: sautéing, tempering, frying and traditional recipes.

Are groundnuts nutritious?

Yes. They provide plant protein, fibre, unsaturated fats, vitamin E, niacin, folate and magnesium, and fit well into a varied, balanced diet.

How should I store the oil and peanuts?

Keep both in airtight containers, in a cool, dry place away from sunlight.

Do your products contain allergens?

Yes. Peanuts are a major allergen. Do not consume if you have a peanut allergy.
